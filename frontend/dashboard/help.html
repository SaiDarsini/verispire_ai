Common Questions

How does verification work?+
Every chat turn runs Planner → Generator → a 5-second subprocess sandbox (when Python is present) → Independent Verifier. The collapsible audit trail shows PASS/FAIL/REJECT, confidence scores, hallucination risk, and execution traces.
How do I enable more agents?+
Go to the Agents page and toggle any agent on or off for your workspace.
How do I reset my password?+
Log out and use "Forgot password?" on the login screen, or change it directly from Settings while logged in.
Where are my uploaded files stored?+
All files live under the Files page and are private to your account.
